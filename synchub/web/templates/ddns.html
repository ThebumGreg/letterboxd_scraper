Where does the delay come from?

After the address changes, three things take time: SyncHub noticing and telling Dynu (every update says how long each step took, below), Dynu's name servers serving the new address, and resolvers that still hold the old answer until its life (TTL) runs out. This check times the first, then asks Dynu's own name servers and the big public resolvers what they say now, so the wait can be put on the right one. It changes nothing.
