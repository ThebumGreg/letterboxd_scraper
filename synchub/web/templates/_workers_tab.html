    Work drive
    
                
    
            
    
                
    
                
    
                
    The worker measures its work folder every five minutes. Only job folders are ever deleted here: a running job and a result SyncHub has not received yet are protected, and anything in the folder that is not SyncHub's is listed as Other and left alone. If a failed job is retried after its files are deleted, the file is copied to the worker again. Kept originals are managed on the Originals page.
