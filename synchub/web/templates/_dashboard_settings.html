Morning TV dashboard

At the start of the morning window SyncHub wakes the TV and opens a full-screen page with the time, weather, forecast, a quote, and a column for each person. At the end it closes the page. See docs/TV_DASHBOARD.md.

Install TV Bro (com.phlox.tvwebbrowser) or another TV browser on the TV. It opens the page full screen.

People

A calendar person shows today's events from their private calendar link (Google: Settings, your calendar, Secret address in iCal format. iCloud: share the calendar publicly and copy the link. Microsoft 365 / Outlook: Settings, Calendar, Shared calendars, Publish a calendar, ICS). A student has a weekly school schedule and optionally a phone to watch on the Wi-Fi.

No school days
One per line, a date or a range, then a name. Students show the name instead of a school day.
A phone must be seen on the Wi-Fi first, then gone this long, so a sleeping phone does not count as leaving. Once someone has left they stay "left" for the day. Phones use a private Wi-Fi address per network: turn that off for your home Wi-Fi (or pick the address UniFi shows, and keep it).
