People

A calendar person shows their events from a private calendar link (Google: Settings, your calendar, Secret address in iCal format. iCloud: share the calendar publicly and copy the link. Microsoft 365 / Outlook: Settings, Calendar, Shared calendars, Publish a calendar, ICS). A student has a weekly school schedule. Either can have a phone on the home Wi-Fi: for a student it shows "Left for school", and for everyone it can start the Welcome home page. Click a person to edit.

Weather and extras
A 5 digit US ZIP code, or latitude and longitude (40.71, -74.00).
Besides the forecast: sunrise and sunset, the moon, the UV index and air quality (free Open-Meteo services), US weather alerts, and lines like "Take an umbrella" or "UV high: sunscreen".
One per line. A full date counts down once; MM-DD repeats every year. The next three within 90 days are shown at the bottom. A line with "birthday" turns the screen (and Welcome home) into a birthday celebration on the day: 03-14 Sam's birthday.

School days for everyone
One per line, a date or a range, then a name. Students show the name instead of a school day. A student's own list and school calendar (inside the student) are added to this.
A phone must be seen on the Wi-Fi first, then gone this long, so a sleeping phone does not count as leaving. Once someone has left they stay "left" for the day. Phones use a private Wi-Fi address per network: turn that off for your home Wi-Fi (or pick the address UniFi shows, and keep it).
Welcome home

When someone's phone joins the home Wi-Fi after being away, the TV shows a full-screen "Welcome home, Sam!" for a couple of minutes, then goes back to what it was. Two people arriving together get one page. On a birthday it says "Happy birthday". It only knows that a phone is on the home Wi-Fi, never where anyone is. Each person needs a phone set under People, and can switch it off for themselves there.
