Radarr and Sonarr tell SyncHub the moment something is grabbed or imported (a webhook), and SyncHub checks download progress while anything is downloading.

Database backups

Every night at 03:30 SyncHub saves a consistent copy of its database (settings, profiles, decisions, everything) in the backups folder beside it, and keeps the newest few. A copy is also saved before an upgrade changes the database. Tools has a button to do it now.

0 turns the nightly copy off.
