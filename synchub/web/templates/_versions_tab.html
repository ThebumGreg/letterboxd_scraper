Versions

Two things for films that are already in the library. A smaller extra copy (for phones and remote viewing) is saved next to the film, and Jellyfin shows it as another version of the same film; the original is never touched. A Dolby Vision 7 to 8.1 conversion fixes big 4K files that almost nothing can play directly, without re-encoding the picture. Nothing happens until you approve it. The list comes from the Library scan, so scan the share first.
